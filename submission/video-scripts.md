# Video scripts

Engagement on the public YouTube demo is scored, so the first 3 seconds decide everything. Record vertical or 16:9, captions on, screen recording with your voice.

## 1-minute demo (Playcast) — about 140 words

**0:00–0:03 HOOK (on camera or big text over the map)**
"Your dishwasher is competing with AI for the same grid, and you pay for it."

**0:03–0:12 Problem, over the 3D map**
"AI data centers cluster in a few hubs. Every evening their load overlaps with yours, and on a time-of-use plan that's your most expensive hour."

**0:12–0:40 Product, screen recording of Ask Sprout**
"This is Sprout. I pick the grid state, my appliances, my utility, and my plan. Gemini turns that into a schedule: dishwasher waits until after 9, EV charges overnight, with reasons."
(Show the plan appear. Flip grid state to Relaxed and ask again so the answer visibly changes.)

**0:40–0:52 The twist**
"Then Sprout writes the same advice two ways: blaming AI demand, or just saying peak hours. That's the research: does naming AI change what people do?"
(Show the two framing cards side by side.)

**0:52–1:00 CTA**
"Try it at marissacodes.com/sprout and join the hardware waitlist."

Recording tips: use the Peak overlap state for the first run (most dramatic), keep the cursor moving, cut every pause.

## 2-minute team intro and pitch — about 280 words

**0:00–0:15 Who**
"I'm Marissa Curry. I'm a software engineer and creator, and I'm applying to PhD programs in AI-energy policy and grid market design."

**0:15–0:40 Problem**
"AI is changing electricity demand faster than anyone can explain it to regular people. Data centers cluster in a handful of hubs, and when their load stacks on top of household demand, time-of-use customers pay peak prices. Nothing connects that to a decision a household can make tonight."

**0:40–1:10 Solution**
"Sprout closes that loop. It shows where the clusters sit, then Gemini turns grid state, your utility, and your plan into a run-now-or-wait schedule for your appliances. And it writes the same advice framed around AI demand versus generic peak hours, so we can test whether naming AI as the cause actually changes behavior."

**1:10–1:30 What's real**
"The map, Ask Sprout, the daily low-carbon AI routing tip, the public research tracker, and the hardware waitlist are all live today. The grid reading comes live from CAISO for California, with a labelled fallback. Next is adding EIA data for Virginia and Texas."

**1:30–1:50 Market**
"FERC Order 2222 is opening wholesale markets to aggregated small flexible loads, and AI is driving load growth. The hardware is the wedge: a smart plug that measures real load and a kitchen display that shows the grid. That turns self-reported behavior into measured data that utilities and aggregators can't easily collect. I build in public, with over 781 thousand views and about 93 percent from non-followers, so I can reach households directly."

**1:50–2:00 Ask**
"I'm pre-revenue and looking for a utility pilot and seed backers who want to own this space. Sprout, at marissacodes.com/sprout."

Before recording, confirm the 781K figure is still current in your Instagram insights, and say only numbers you can show.
