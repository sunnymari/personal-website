The project in four questions

Who

Who it's for

Robotics technicians, field service engineers, and controls teams who get called when a robot stops moving. It's also for anyone curious how a robot "knows" something is wrong. I designed, built, and modeled Starbot end to end.

What

What it is

A diagnostics tool for a 28-actuator robot. A Python simulator sends 8-byte CAN frames. A decoder turns them into readings, and a rules-based detector debounces faults into diagnostic trouble codes with freeze-frame evidence. A three.js viewer shows every joint's health on a 3D model.

Where

Where it fits

Modeled on the CAN-bus networks inside humanoids, cobots, and factory arms. The data here is synthetic. The full Python virtual-CAN source is on GitHub, and the demo above runs as a browser simulation, so no hardware is needed.

Why

Why it matters

When a robot faults, most of the downtime goes into finding the problem, not fixing it. Raw bus logs are hard to read under pressure. Starbot links each colored joint to the reading, threshold, and moment that triggered it. The goal is to turn "something's wrong" into "left knee, overcurrent, here's the proof."

Where Starbot is useful

Starbot's value is the monitoring layer, not the robot body. Any robot with actuators needs one, whether it's a warehouse robot or a humanoid.

Factory

End-of-line testing

Run every joint through its checks before a robot ships, and flag the ones that fail with the exact reading and threshold.

Fleet

Fleet health monitoring

Show which robots and joints are drifting toward a fault, so repairs can be scheduled before a breakdown stops the line.

Service

Faster repairs

Point a technician straight to the failing joint with the evidence attached, so the right part gets replaced the first time.

Engineering

Root cause

Trigger values, freeze-frames, and fault history help engineers find patterns across failures and fix them in the next design.

Try a tiny diagnostics adventure

01

Make a little trouble

Select left knee and choose Overcurrent spike. Watch its marker turn yellow while the detector checks, then red when the fault is confirmed.

02

Follow the evidence

Click the joint or its alert. Read the measured value, threshold, trigger time, and freeze-frame. Try Drop heartbeat on a wrist to see a different kind of fault.

03

Help Starbot recover

Choose Reset all injections. Healthy samples heal the faults while the history stays visible. Pause connection demonstrates stale data and automatic recovery.
