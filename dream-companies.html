Tesla

#1 on the list

Why Tesla: mission and culture that match my sustainability passion, abundance through tech, and energy infrastructure that keeps up with AI.

Sprout talking points

  • Impact at scale. Tesla’s 2025 Impact Report: customers kept nearly 37 million metric tons of CO₂e out of the air, about the annual energy use of 5 million homes.
  • Mission = abundance + sustainability. Building amazing abundance across the product line, with sustainability and technology going hand in hand.
  • AI meets the grid. The report treats AI-powered data centers as a real variable in Tesla’s own energy story, and notes Megapack alone avoided about 470,000 metric tons of CO₂e globally in 2025 by supporting the grid.
  • Same tension Sprout was built for. Sprout maps U.S. data center clusters and gives grid-stress guidance for when to run appliances: a consumer-facing take on the problem Tesla solves at grid scale with Megapack and storage.
  • Why I want to be there. I’m drawn to teams solving AI + energy demand at the source, not just writing about the headlines.
